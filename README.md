# Guardian of the Milky Way
Guardian of the Milky Way is a fun web based space shooter game. Fly missions, earn credits, and more in the ultimate space shooter. Features autosave to your account. Play *completely free* at [the website](https://guardianofthemilkyway.netlify.app)!
## How to play (To be completed)
To create an account, simply enter in your email and a password you'll remember, then click "Create Account." Then you can sign in. That's it! If you'd prefer, you can continue as a guest, but all your progress will be lost upon closing/refreshing the page. Click "enter hangar" to open the mission select/upgrade menu. Select a mission from the roster (Only Training Run is available from the start). The mission objective and reward will be shown. Before launching the mission, make sure you understand the objective, as every mission is different. Once you've started, use W to thrust forward and A/D to steer. Alternatively, you can use the arrow keys. Press Space to shoot, and once you unlock Missiles, press Shift to fire them. Both attacks can be fired repetitively by holding down their respective buttons, but note that missiles have a cooldown.
