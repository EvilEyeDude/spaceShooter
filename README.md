# Guardian of the Milky Way
Guardian of the Milky Way is a fun web based space shooter game. Fly missions, earn credits, and more in the ultimate space shooter. Features autosave to your account. Play *completely free* at [the website](https://guardianofthemilkyway.netlify.app)!
## How to play
To create an account, simply enter in your email and a password you'll remember, then click "Create Account." Then you can sign in. That's it! If you'd prefer, you can continue as a guest, but all your progress will be lost upon closing/refreshing the page. Click "enter hangar" to open the mission select/upgrade menu. Select a mission from the roster (Only Training Run is available from the start). The mission objective and reward will be shown. Before launching the mission, make sure you understand the objective, as every mission is different. Once you've started, use W to thrust forward and A/D to turn. Alternatively, you can use the arrow keys. Press Space to shoot, and once you unlock Missiles, press Shift to fire them. Both attacks can be fired repetitively by holding down their respective buttons, but note that missiles have a cooldown. Once you complete a mission, the next in the list will be unlocked. You can fight with other players! Simply click "PVP arena" as soon as you login. Unfortunately, the player base is quite small as of now so you might have to get a friend to play with you. Note that the arena is still a bit buggy.
